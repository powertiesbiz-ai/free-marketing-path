# The Free Marketing Path

*by [powertiesbiz](https://github.com/powertiesbiz-ai)*

A step-by-step guided path that walks a small-business owner through marketing their business for **free — or as close to free as honestly possible** — by setting up the exact system PowerTies runs today: website, SEO, AI-search visibility, content, a quiz funnel, booking and payment, CRM + automation, reviews, community, partnerships, measured outreach, and niche research.

No hype, no $2,000 course, no "growth hacks." Just the real stack, the real tools, and the real numbers.

Built and run by a business owner who doesn't code — directed in plain language, executed and verified by an AI ring leader; Tank runs on Keith's Muse access at $20/month — but the ring-leader ROLE costs $0 to fill: wear the hat yourself, or staff it with a free open-source agent. If you can describe what you want, you can run this system.

## Who it's for

Solo owners and small teams who do good work and are invisible online — and who would rather spend a weekend building a system than spend money they don't have on ads that stop the day the budget does.

## The promise

**$0 or near-$0 at every step.** Where PowerTies spends a little, we say so plainly: our entire all-in spend is ≈$56/mo — a ≈$15/mo small server that runs our automation (Step 7), a ≈$21/mo Claude plan that speeds up lead research (Step 11), and $20/mo for the Muse access our AI ring leader (Tank) runs on — and every one of those has a $0 alternative (run n8n on your own computer, do the research manually, fill the ring-leader role yourself or with a free open-source agent). The full ledger is in the [proof log](docs/proof-log.md). If a step ever costs real money, the doc says how much and why.

## The path

1. **Website — your home base** — A fast, credible home base on the internet that you own and control.
2. **Get found — Google Business Profile & Search Console** — Show up when someone nearby searches for what you do.
3. **AI-search visibility** — Be findable — and quotable — when people ask AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google's AI answers) for a recommendation.
4. **Content system** — Publish useful content on a rhythm you can sustain — without it eating your week.
5. **Lead magnet + quiz funnel** — Give strangers a free, useful first step that naturally points to your paid offer.
6. **Booking + payment** — Let a ready buyer book and pay without a single back-and-forth email.
7. **CRM + automation** — Every lead, reply, and customer tracked in one place — software does the busywork, and a human approves anything that leaves the building.
8. **Reviews + directories** — A steady trickle of fresh reviews, and your business listed accurately everywhere that's free.
9. **Community** — Be a known, helpful regular in the places your buyers already hang out.
10. **Partnerships & barter** — Trade skill, audience, and trust instead of cash — grow through other people's customers and capabilities.
11. **Outreach + measurement** — A small, honest, measurable flow of new conversations — and the discipline to know whether it's working.
12. **Find your niche and research the solution** — Aim the whole system at one niche: learn its real problems, in their own words, from open resources, AI, and the forums where they talk — then join the conversation.

The full blueprint — goals, why each step matters, exact tools, costs, time estimates, checklists, and how PowerTies does it — lives in **[docs/the-path.md](docs/the-path.md)**.

## Use the interactive UI

**Live site: [powertiesbiz-ai.github.io/free-marketing-path](https://powertiesbiz-ai.github.io/free-marketing-path/)** — or open **[index.html](index.html)** in a browser. Walk the path step by step: each step expands to its goal, tools, cost, time, and checklist. Check items off as you complete them and watch the progress bar move.

The site also carries the funnel live, exactly as Step 5 teaches it: the path points to **[MLOT](mlot.html)** — our free Money Left on the Table diagnostic that finds where a business is losing time and money — and MLOT points to **[the $99 AI working session](ai-session.html)**, the paid offer that designs the fix. Your progress is saved **only in your own browser** (localStorage). There is no account, no database, and nothing about you is collected — ever. There's a "Reset progress" button when you want a clean slate.
