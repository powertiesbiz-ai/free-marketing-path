What MLOT does

MLOT looks at how your business actually runs — the repetitive work, the follow-ups that slip through, the hours eaten by tasks a tool could carry — and shows you where the money is leaking out.

What you get is a snapshot of your own business: where time and money are being lost, and how big each leak looks. Your gaps, in your numbers — not generic advice.

What MLOT doesn't do

MLOT is a diagnostic, pure and simple: it finds the leaks. It does not prescribe the fix. Deciding which tools to adopt, in what order, and what each one is worth to your business is a design job — that's exactly what the $99 AI working session is for.

Who it's for

Small business owners who suspect they're leaving money on the table but can't see precisely where. If you run the business and feel the drag of work that shouldn't need you, MLOT was built for you.
