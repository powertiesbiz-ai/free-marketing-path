# Website launch checklist

A distilled go-live list from Steps 1 and 2 of the path. “Published” is not the same as ready — test the site like a stranger on a phone.

## Build and hosting

- [ ] Site is hosted on a free static host (Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, or equivalent).
- [ ] The source is in a repo or another backed-up location you control.
- [ ] Custom domain is connected, if you are using one.
- [ ] HTTPS works on the domain and the www/non-www version resolves the way you intend.
- [ ] No page contains placeholder text, test prices, or someone else's example copy.

## Core pages

- [ ] Home page says what you do, who it is for, where you work, and what to do next.
- [ ] About page identifies the real business and builds trust without inventing credentials.
- [ ] Offer page states the service, the outcome, the process, and the price or next step where appropriate.
- [ ] Contact page gives a working contact method and the lead-capture form.
- [ ] Privacy/terms pages are present if your forms, analytics, or business require them.
- [ ] Page titles and descriptions say plainly what each page is about.

## Visitor test

- [ ] Open every page on a phone.
- [ ] Check text size, buttons, menus, images, and forms at mobile width.
- [ ] Click every navigation link and every call-to-action.
- [ ] Submit the lead form with a test entry and confirm it arrives where it should.
- [ ] Confirm the confirmation message or next step after submission is clear.
- [ ] Test the booking and payment links without completing a real purchase.
- [ ] Check the site in a private/incognito window, signed out of your accounts.

## Get found

- [ ] Google Business Profile is claimed or created.
- [ ] Business name, categories, hours, services, service area, phone, website, and photos are complete.
- [ ] Business name, address/location wording, and phone are consistent with the website and major listings.
- [ ] Google Search Console ownership is verified.
- [ ] `sitemap.xml` exists, lists the public pages you want indexed, and has been submitted in Search Console.
- [ ] The site has no accidental `noindex` on pages you want found.
- [ ] Important pages load without a login and return successfully to a signed-out visitor.

## First-week follow-up

- [ ] Ask one person who does not know the business to find the offer and contact you — watch where they get stuck.
- [ ] Fix any broken link, confusing label, or form problem the test exposed.
- [ ] Record the launch date and baseline in your proof log.
- [ ] Add the next step: FAQ/AI-search visibility (Step 3), not a redesign.
