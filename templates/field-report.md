# Field report — one real attempt, reported straight

A field report is the data this project runs on: one real thing you tried, what it cost in cash and hours, and what actually happened — in numbers where you have them. Wins and flops both count. This template is how community answers become research instead of anecdotes (see the [research questions](../docs/thesis-and-research.md) your report helps answer).

**How to submit:** open a GitHub issue with this template filled in, or post it in the community channels (Reddit / Discord, as powertiesbiz) and say it's a field report.

## Consent — required, pick one

We never publish a business name without the reporter's explicit choice. Check exactly one:

- [ ] **Publish with my business name** — you may use my business name and town.
- [ ] **Publish anonymized** — trade and town/region only, no business or personal name.
- [ ] **Do not publish** — research use only; my numbers may inform the project's conclusions but the report itself stays private.

## What counts

A report of a **flop** is as valuable as a win — negative results are half the dataset, and the half nobody else publishes. "I posted in three Facebook groups every week for a month, spent six hours, got zero calls" is a genuinely useful field report. So is "my Google profile brought me two jobs and I did nothing else." Report what happened, not what was supposed to happen.

## EXAMPLE — PowerTies outreach baseline *(a real report, labeled as an example of the format)*

- **Consent:** Publish with business name (PowerTies).
- **Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Business type / trade:** Business consulting
- **Town / region:** Queens, NY
- **Tactic tried:** Step 11 — outreach (scored lead list, verified emails, small daily batches)
- **Cash spent:** ≈$0 attributable to the tactic, out of a total stack of ≈$36/month
- **Hours spent:** Not separately tracked for this baseline period — which is itself one of the things this project is trying to start measuring (research question 3)
- **What exactly was done:** Built a lead queue of founder/CEO contacts at small US businesses, scored each lead for fit, verified email addresses before sending, sent personalized outreach emails in batches of about 10 per weekday starting October 1, 2026, and logged every send, bounce, and reply.
- **Result:** 79 sent / 55 delivered / 27 bounced / **0 replies, $0 revenue** as of October 13, 2026.
- **What we'd do differently:** The bounce count exposed the hidden failure: guessed email addresses from an earlier version of the list-building process. Verification is now mandatory before any send — no guessed addresses, ever. The honest 30-day checkpoint for this tactic is October 31, 2026.
- **May we follow up?** Yes.
