# Review request email

Send this soon after the work is finished and the customer has seen the result. Keep it short. One Google link and, when relevant, one niche-directory link are enough.

**Subject options:**

- Quick favor, [First name]?
- Would you share how it went?

---

Hi [First name],

Thanks again for trusting [Business name] with [the project/service]. I'm glad we could [specific result or completed item, if true].

Would you be willing to leave a short Google review? It helps another [customer type] know what to expect, and it genuinely helps a small business like ours get found:

[Google review link]

If you also use [niche directory name], a review there helps too — but Google alone is plenty:

[Niche directory review link]

Honest and specific is best. One or two sentences about what we did and how it went is more useful than a paragraph.

Thanks again,

[Your name]  
[Business name]  
[Phone or best contact]

---

## Sending notes

- Replace or delete the niche-directory paragraph if you do not have a relevant second listing.
- Do not offer a discount, gift, or other incentive in exchange for a review.
- Do not ask only happy customers and route unhappy customers elsewhere. Ask consistently, learn from criticism, and respond professionally.
- Test both links on your phone before using the template.
